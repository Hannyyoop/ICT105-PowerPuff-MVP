# Digital Go-to-Market Plan

## Product / Prototype Name

Study Room Booking System


## Target Early Users

The first users are university students who frequently use study rooms for individual study, group assignments, and club meetings. Library staff will also use the system to manage room bookings and monitor availability.


## Main Value Proposition

The Study Room Booking System helps students quickly find and reserve available study rooms online, saving time and making room management more efficient.

## Channel Plan

| Channel | Target User | Message Angle | User Action Expected | Metric to Track | Why This Channel Fits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| University class chat group | Students | Book study rooms anytime without visiting the library | Click the demo link | Demo link clicks | Most students communicate through class chat groups |
| Campus poster with QR code | Students on campus | Scan to check room availability and make a booking | Scan QR code | QR code scans | Students see posters near the library and study areas |
| Instagram story | University students | Easy and convenient study room booking | Visit the landing page | Story views and link clicks | Students frequently use Instagram |
| University website | Students and staff | Manage study room bookings online | Open the prototype | Website visits | Official platform used by students and staff |

## Launch Sequence

1. Prepare the landing page and prototype demo link.
2. Share the prototype through class chat groups, campus QR posters, Instagram, and the university website.
3. Track user activity using Google Sheets or prototype logs.
4. Collect user feedback through a Google Form.
5. Improve the prototype and update features based on user feedback.

## Risk and Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Students may not notice the prototype. | Promote it through multiple channels such as class chat groups, posters, Instagram, and the university website. |
| Users may experience bugs or errors. | Test the prototype before sharing and fix reported issues quickly. |
| Users may be concerned about privacy. | Collect only necessary booking information and use sample or masked data during testing. |
| Low participation in user testing. | Encourage classmates and library staff to test the prototype and provide feedback. |
