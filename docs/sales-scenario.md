## 1. Project Title

Study Room Booking System

## 2. Target User / Customer

- Primary user: University students
- Secondary user: Library staff
- Decision-maker or admin role, if any: Administrator

## 3. Situation / Context

A student needs a quiet study room to prepare for an exam or complete a group assignment. When they visit the library, they are unsure which rooms are available and have to ask the library staff or check manually. This takes time and may result in waiting or booking conflicts.

## 4. Pain Point

Students spend too much time checking room availability and making reservations. The manual booking process can cause confusion, double bookings, and delays. Library staff also spend extra time managing bookings manually.

## 5. Product Introduction

The Study Room Booking System is introduced as a web-based platform that allows students to check available study rooms, make reservations, view booking details, and track booking status online. Library staff can manage bookings and update reservation status through the admin page.

## 6. Value Message

For university students who experience difficulty finding and booking available study rooms, our Study Room Booking System helps them reserve study rooms quickly and conveniently by providing online booking, room availability checking, booking status tracking, and an admin management system.

## 7. Sales Conversation / Pitch Moment

"Do you spend time looking for an available study room or waiting to ask library staff? Our Study Room Booking System allows you to check room availability, book a study room, and view your booking status online. It saves time, reduces booking conflicts, and makes the booking process simple and convenient."

## 8. Expected User Action

- Try the demo
- Book a study room
- Check room availability
- Give feedback
- Share the system with other students
