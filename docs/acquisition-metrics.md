## Purpose

Define how the group will measure early user interest and the performance of the Study Room Booking System landing page.

## Required Metrics

| Metric ID | Metric Name | What It Measures | Formula / Counting Method | Data Source | Target / Success Criterion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M-01 | Landing page views | Number of users who visited the landing page | Count total page visits | Landing page / spreadsheet | At least 20 page views |
| M-02 | CTA clicks | Number of users who clicked the "Book a Study Room" button | CTA clicks ÷ page views × 100 | Landing page | At least 30% click rate |
| M-03 | Demo attempts | Number of users who opened or tested the prototype | Count prototype visits | Prototype link / Google Sheets | At least 10 demo attempts |
| M-04 | Feedback responses | Number of users who submitted feedback | Count completed feedback forms | Google Form / spreadsheet | At least 5 responses |
| M-05 | Booking attempts | Percentage of users who completed a study room booking | Booking attempts ÷ page views × 100 | Prototype / spreadsheet | At least 20% booking rate |

## Interpretation

If the results do not meet the target, the group will:

- Improve the landing page headline to better explain the system.
- Make the call-to-action button more visible and attractive.
- Simplify the booking process by reducing unnecessary steps.
- Share the prototype through more student communication channels.
- Improve the explanation of the benefits of the Study Room Booking System based on user feedback.
